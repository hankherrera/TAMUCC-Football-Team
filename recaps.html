Season Recaps

Team by team, season by season — built from box scores and each year's final finish.

Team GuetzkeCooper Guetzke
10–4 · 1st

Cooper Guetzke stormed to a 9–1 start before the wheels nearly came off — a four-game losing streak in Weeks 10–13 briefly threatened the top seed. A Week 14 win over Hanky's Hitters steadied the ship at 10–4 and the 1-seed, and Team Guetzke made it count in the postseason, running the table through all three playoff rounds — including a coin-flip championship over Hanky's Hitters — for the franchise's first title.

Hanky's HittersHank Herrera
9–5 · 2nd

Hank Herrera's squad won three of its first four games, weathered a mid-season rough patch, and reeled off wins in Weeks 11–12 to reach 9–3. Back-to-back late losses to Deshaun Don't Touchdown There and Team Guetzke dropped the finish to 9–5, but Hanky's Hitters carried the 2-seed all the way to the championship, coming up just short in a literal tie against Team Guetzke.

Skyler football teamSkyler Sills
8–6 · 3rd

Skyler Sills started 0–2, ripped off five wins in six weeks to reach 6–4, then hit a two-game skid in Weeks 11–12. A pair of Week 13–14 wins closed the year at 8–6 and the 3-seed, setting up a playoff run that ended in a Round 2 loss to Hanky's Hitters.

Sticky fingersLance Williams
8–6 · 4th

Sticky fingers bounced between hot and cold most of the year, including a 0.50-point nail-biter win over Hanky's Hitters in Week 7. A Week 11 loss to Team Garcia was followed by back-to-back wins to reach 8–5, before a Week 14 blowout loss to team big time closed the regular season at 8–6. The playoff run ended one win short of the championship, falling to Team Guetzke in Round 2.

Deshaun Don't Touchdown ThereGrayson Klanika
7–7 · 5th

Grayson Klanika's team looked like a contender early, dropping a league-high 166.52 points in a Week 3 win, then survived a five-week cold stretch through the middle of the season. A three-game winning streak in Weeks 11–13 — including a win over Hanky's Hitters — pulled the season back to .500 before a Week 14 loss to Team Garcia settled it at 7–7 and the 5-seed. The playoff run ended in Round 1 against Sticky fingers.

team big timeNolan S

Jay's Black GuysJake Herrera

Jake Herrera's squad had its moments, including a Week 11 upset of Team Guetzke, and pushed its record to 6–6 by Week 12. A pair of close late losses — 124.86–128.74 to Sticky fingers and 119.14–121.14 to Skyler football team — closed the year at 6–8, outside the playoff field.

Above and bijanedMax Aynesworth

Ceedeez NutsConnor Jones
5–9 · 9th

The 5–9 finish left Ceedeez Nuts outside the playoff picture.

Team GarciaCamden Garcia

A Week 14 win over Deshaun Don't Touchdown There was one of only four wins all year, closing the campaign at 4–10 and outside the playoffs.

SkybreezeSkyler Sills
9–6 · 4th (reg. season), Champion

Skyler Sills opened 4–0 and looked like the class of the league before a two-game skid in Weeks 5–6. Skybreeze bounced back to reach 9–6 despite a rough patch in Weeks 11–12, closing the regular season on a Week 15 win. The playoffs were a different story entirely — Skybreeze blitzed through all three rounds, capped by a runaway 134.02–78.84 win in the championship, for the franchise's first title.

PlisskenJames Spitz
8–7 · 6th (reg. season), Runner-up

James Spitz dropped a big Week 1 win, then hit the skids hard — six losses in the next seven weeks. But Plissken caught fire late, winning six of the last seven regular-season games, including a 190.38-point outburst in Week 14 (the second-most combined points ever recorded, at 335.14). A brutal Week 15 finish — 34.80 points, an all-time league low, in a 91.4-point blowout loss — was forgotten fast, as Plissken carried the momentum through the playoffs all the way to the championship before falling to Skybreeze.

Schim ReaperColin Schimcek
10–4 · 3rd (reg. season), Champion

Colin Schimcek's debut season was a statement from the jump — an 8–2 start through Week 10 included a 194.1-point explosion in Week 9 (a 111.1-point blowout over Camden Garcia, the largest margin of victory in league history) and a 0.06-point nail-biter over John Williams in Week 8, the closest game on record. A two-game slide to close the regular season barely dented the résumé; Schim Reaper still finished 10–4 and the 3-seed, then ran through the playoffs — including a rematch win over NoloRizzGang — for the title, a champion in his first year in the league.
